Planning Your Inspection

How Niles Homebuyers Can Prepare for an Inspection

Niles offers older river-town houses, established neighborhoods, and mid-century residences. Reviewing the specific property's updates and visible condition is more useful than assuming defects based only on its construction era.

Older homes and renovation records

Ask about the ages of the roof, heating equipment, plumbing, and electrical improvements. Accessible equipment and visible wiring conditions can be evaluated, but a home inspection cannot establish the condition of every concealed component.

Basement moisture and lot drainage

For a property near the St. Joseph River or with an older basement, ask sellers about prior seepage, grading work, and foundation repairs. Inspectors document visible observations but do not certify flood resistance or predict future water entry.

Weather and system testing

During colder Michigan weather, an air conditioner may not be operated when testing could be unreliable or risk equipment damage. Any limitation should be recorded, and the buyer can arrange appropriate follow-up testing when conditions permit.
