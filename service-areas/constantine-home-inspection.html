Planning Your Inspection

Buying in Constantine: Inspection Considerations

Constantine includes established village homes, St. Joseph River-area properties, and residences farther outside town. The property’s location can raise useful questions, but it does not establish that a defect is present.

Basements and exterior drainage

Ask about past basement water entry and improvements to gutters, downspouts, and site grading. The inspector can document visible moisture indicators and accessible foundation surfaces but cannot determine flood risk or guarantee a dry basement.

Older installations and alterations

When viewing an older house, ask whether electrical, plumbing, roof, or heating upgrades have been completed and documented. A home inspection can identify observed defects but does not verify concealed work or establish compliance with current codes.

Wood-destroying organisms

If you need information about termite or other wood-destroying organism activity, request a separate WDO inspection. This service is not automatically included with a standard visual home inspection and may have its own access and reporting requirements.

If a home is close to the river or has a history of moisture entry, share that information when scheduling. Request an inspection or view all service areas.
