Why Three Rivers Trusts Lighthouse

Lighthouse Property Inspections is headquartered right here in Three Rivers — this is our home. We know the neighborhoods, the builders, and the unique characteristics of the local housing stock. Three Rivers sits at the confluence of the St. Joseph, Rocky, and Portage rivers, and that proximity to water means we pay special attention to moisture intrusion, grading, and drainage around every property we inspect.

Much of Three Rivers' charm comes from its pre-war craftsman-style homes and mid-century bungalows that line the streets near downtown and along the river. These homes have character, but they also come with common findings we see regularly: aging foundations with mortar deterioration, outdated electrical panels, original plumbing that may be nearing the end of its service life, and roofing systems that take a beating from harsh Michigan winters. We know exactly what to look for because we inspect these homes year-round.

Planning Your Inspection

Preparing for a Three Rivers Home Inspection

From older houses near downtown to properties farther outside the city center, inspection priorities depend on the home itself—not just its age or location.

Water entry and drainage

Ask about previous basement water intrusion, drainage improvements, and foundation repairs, especially if a property has lower-level living space. A standard inspection records visible conditions but cannot predict future flooding or uncover concealed moisture.

Older systems and renovations

Request available records for roof replacement, electrical improvements, plumbing updates, and heating equipment. Accessible components are assessed on inspection day; an inspection cannot guarantee their remaining service life.

Radon measurements

Radon testing is separate from the visual home inspection. Lighthouse offers continuous radon measurements that ordinarily require a 48-hour testing period, so coordinate testing with your purchase timeline.

Make sure utilities are on and the attic, basement, and mechanical equipment can be accessed when applicable. Request an inspection or view all service areas.
